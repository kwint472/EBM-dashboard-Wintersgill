## Why Research-Supported Interventions Fail in Practice - Practitioner Failure Analysis

**Purpose:** Practitioners reveal failure patterns through trial-and-error that research studies don't capture (researchers publish successes, not failures).

### FAILURE MODE 1: Training Without Accountability
- **What Happened:** Leaders receive transformational leadership training but no consequences for not changing behaviors; go through motions without real change
- **Practitioner Evidence:** NYC H+H Cineas emphasizes "learning curve" for leaders to actually LISTEN to frontline (implies initial resistance and need for ongoing reinforcement beyond training)
- **Why Science Missed This:** Study 3 small voluntary pilot (N=10) had self-selected motivated participants; doesn't capture resistant supervisors in mandatory scaled implementations
- **Federal Adaptation:** 360-degree feedback MUST tie to performance evaluations; can't rely on training alone given civil service protections preventing termination; need coaching + consequences + public accountability

### FAILURE MODE 2: Top-Down Without Frontline Input
- **What Happened:** "Programs implemented without meaningful nurse input proving unsustainable" (NYC H+H); leaders designed programs workers didn't value
- **Practitioner Evidence:** Cineas explicitly identifies this as failed approach before structural empowerment; initial programs ignored frontline perspectives
- **Why Science Missed This:** Research studies designed by academics, not co-designed with frontline workers; participation often voluntary (selection bias toward those who like the approach)
- **Federal Adaptation:** Governance councils, union partnership, frontline co-design NON-NEGOTIABLE; federal workers especially skeptical after "another program" failures (33.9% feel betrayed per FEVS)

### FAILURE MODE 3: One-Time Wellness Programs
- **What Happened:** "One-time wellness programs without sustained cultural change" (Feist) don't address organizational drivers; yoga classes and resilience training without fixing toxic leadership, workload, or institutional betrayal
- **Practitioner Evidence:** Feist identifies this as common failed approach practitioners learned through trial-and-error
- **Why Science Missed This:** Study 1 shows organizational > individual factors but doesn't explicitly test wellness program failures longitudinally
- **Federal Adaptation:** AVOID supplementing leadership training with wellness programs that send implicit message "burnout is your personal problem to manage not organizational problem to fix"

### FAILURE MODE 4: Insufficient Executive Sponsorship
- **What Happened:** "Insufficient leadership engagement and lack of follow-through after initial programs" (Feist); programs fade when initial enthusiasm wanes or executive champion leaves
- **Practitioner Evidence:** ALL practitioners unanimous C-suite commitment essential; Cineas "Dr. Katz as #1 supporter"; Feist emphasizes championship as "necessity not nicety"
- **Why Science Missed This:** Study 3 had PI/faculty champion throughout 3-month study; doesn't capture what happens when champion leaves or organizational priorities shift
- **Federal Adaptation:** Facility directors, CNOs, hospital commanders HELD ACCOUNTABLE for FEVS outcomes in their performance evaluations; not HR-led "nice to have" initiative

### FAILURE MODE 5: Unrealistic Timeline / Expecting Quick Results
- **What Happened:** Organizations implement intervention, assess at 3-6 months, conclude "not working," abandon before cultural change solidifies
- **Practitioner Evidence:** NYC H+H 6-year journey (2019-present); Ohio State multi-year; ALL emphasize "cultural change takes years not months"
- **Why Science Missed This:** Studies publish at 3-month follow-up (Study 3 max); researchers don't capture long-term sustainability or see abandonment after initial disappointing results
- **Federal Adaptation:** RESIST organizational pressure for immediate results; leading indicators (training completion Month 3-9, behavior change Month 6-12) build confidence while awaiting lagging outcomes (burnout Month 12-24, turnover Month 12-24)

### FAILURE MODE 6: Not Addressing Workplace Violence/Incivility
- **What Happened:** Focus on leadership training alone without addressing workplace violence, incivility ($2 billion/day cost Feist cites), bullying creating unsafe environment
- **Practitioner Evidence:** Feist explicitly identifies "failing to address workplace violence and incivility as burnout drivers" as common pitfall practitioners observe
- **Why Science Missed This:** Studies 1-5 don't measure workplace violence explicitly; Study 2 institutional betrayal captures some but not physical safety threats
- **Federal Adaptation:** Leadership training curriculum must include de-escalation, violence prevention, safe reporting systems; especially VA (veterans with PTSD/TBI), IHS (safety concerns in remote areas)

### FAILURE MODE 7: Invasive Credentialing Creating Help-Seeking Barriers
- **What Happened:** Organizations implement mental health support BUT credentialing asks "have you ever been diagnosed/treated for mental illness" deterring help-seeking; workers fear license loss
- **Practitioner Evidence:** Feist identifies as major barrier; CNOs must report to licensing boards creating institutional betrayal; workers see contradiction between "we support you" messaging and "tell us so we can report you" reality
- **Why Science Missed This:** Studies 1-5 don't examine credentialing processes or licensing barriers; researchers assume workers will use available resources if offered
- **Federal Adaptation:** Federal agencies must audit credentialing questions (OPM security clearances, medical licensing, professional certifications); leadership training alone insufficient if institutional barriers remain; policy changes required

**PRACTITIONER WISDOM FOR FEDERAL IMPLEMENTATION:**

**"Research shows leadership training CAN work (Study 3 proof-of-concept). Practitioners show WHEN it DOESN'T work and how to avoid those failures:**
1. ✅ Training + accountability (360-feedback tied to performance evals) NOT training alone
2. ✅ Co-design with frontline + unions NOT top-down mandates
3. ✅ Cultural transformation NOT one-time wellness programs
4. ✅ C-suite held accountable for outcomes NOT HR-led nice-to-have
5. ✅ 3-6 year timeline with early leading indicators NOT 3-6 month quick fix
6. ✅ Comprehensive safety (violence prevention, incivility policies) NOT leadership training alone
7. ✅ Remove credentialing barriers through policy NOT just offer support services

**Federal implementation incorporating practitioner failure lessons has HIGHER probability of success than naive application of Study 3 research protocol."**
